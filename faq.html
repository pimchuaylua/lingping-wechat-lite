Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Lingping

From how sessions work to which city to join — here's what our community asks us most.

How do I join?

Simply get a membership or day pass, book your first session, and show up!

Is Lingping a language class?

No! We're not a language school or a traditional language class.

We're a community of people who are curious about languages, cultures, and the world, coming together to have interesting conversations and learn from each other.

Where can I join?

🇭🇰 Hong Kong
💻 Online

And many more cities to come!

How do in-person sessions work?

The host introduces a topic and guides the conversation. We take turns sharing our thoughts, experiences, and perspectives.

The main goal is to learn from each other and have a meaningful conversation.

Language is simply the bridge.

If you're still learning the language of the session, don't worry. Our hosts and native/fluent speakers are there to help when you get stuck or don't understand something.

How do online sessions work?

Pretty much the same as in-person sessions — just online!

A small group, a topic, and lots of conversation. 🌏

What are the discussions about?

Every session has a different topic chosen by the host. It can be anything from food, travel, childhood, and lifestyle to AI, technology, philosophy, and more.

We talk about things we're genuinely curious about.

How many people are in each session?

Maximum 6 people per group. Strictly.

We're proud of keeping Lingping small and cozy. We're not a big meetup where you meet 30 people, exchange a few "Hi, how are you?"s, and move on.

Instead, we get together in small groups to have deeper conversations, explore meaningful questions, and genuinely get to know the people around us.

It's this small-group setting that creates the comfort and trust that makes Lingping feel different — a place where people can open up, share different perspectives, and leave having learned something new about the world and each other.

Who are the hosts? Are they teachers?

Our members take turns hosting sessions and choosing topics.

We're not a language school, so our hosts aren't necessarily teachers. They're simply interesting people with different backgrounds and professions — from engineers and scientists to students, lawyers, entrepreneurs, and more.

We believe everyone has something to teach and something to learn.

Who are the members?

Lingping members come from all over the world.

In each city, we try to bring together a good mix of locals and foreigners — people born and raised in the city alongside people from America, Europe, Asia, and beyond.

The result is a community where you can hear different perspectives, cultures, and ways of thinking — all in the same conversation.

My English / Thai / Chinese isn't very good. Can I join?

Yes! There's no such thing as "bad" — you're just still learning. 🌱

We split sessions by language and level, so you can choose the language and level you feel comfortable with — or the level you want to challenge yourself with.

You don't need to speak perfectly. The goal is to communicate, not to be perfect. As long as you can get your point across and understand each other, you're communicating — and that's what matters.

  • City + Online Membership — join in-person sessions in your city + all online sessions
  • Online Only Membership — join our online sessions from anywhere
  • Day Passes — try individual sessions
