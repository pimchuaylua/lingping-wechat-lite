Community Rules

How we keep Lingping sessions great

A few simple guidelines on behavior, booking, and cancellations so every session stays small, respectful, and reliable for everyone.

🏡 Community Behavior Guidelines

  • Be on time. Arriving late can disrupt the discussion and group flow.
  • Respect others and be polite. Be considerate and respectful of everyone.
  • Respect the free space for ideas. Disagree respectfully.
  • Be a good listener. Let others finish speaking and give everyone a chance to participate.
  • Give constructive feedback. Share thoughts in a helpful and encouraging way.
  • Encourage inclusivity and positivity. Create a space where everyone feels valued.

📅 Booking & Cancellation Guidelines

  • Book sessions in advance. Bookings are required to maintain small, high-quality discussions and help hosts manage their time better.
  • Support our café venues. Please order at least one drink during the session.
  • No walk-ins or unbooked guests. Only booked participants may join sessions. Booking is still required even if you only want to sit in without participating.
  • Cancel early if you can’t join. Please cancel at least 3 hours before the session so others can take your spot and hosts can plan better.

⏰ Late Cancellation & No Show Policy

Effective Monday, October 12, 2026

We understand that plans can change. Our sessions have limited spots, so cancelling early helps us offer your spot to someone else and helps our hosts plan ahead.

How do penalty points work?

  • Late cancellation — 1 point: Cancelling less than 3 hours before the session counts as a late cancellation.
  • No-show — 2 points: Booking a session but not attending without cancelling beforehand counts as a no-show.

What happens when you receive penalty points?

  • 1–2 points: We’ll send you a friendly reminder about the cancellation policy.
  • 3 points: Your account may be temporarily restricted from booking sessions for 3 days.
  • Further late cancellations or no-shows: May result in a longer temporary booking restriction.

A little flexibility

Penalty points are simply there to help keep bookings fair and sessions reliable for everyone. We understand that genuine emergencies can happen, so if something unexpected comes up, please let us know.

🚫 Unacceptable Behavior

  • Using impolite language, aggressive behavior, or intimidation.
  • Attacking, discriminating against, or bullying others based on nationality, background, age, gender, or personal identity.
  • Disrespecting others’ ideas or repeatedly interrupting or dominating conversations.
  • Harassing others through unwanted messages or repeated contact.
  • Spreading rumors, gossip, or false information about others in the community.
  • Making inappropriate comments or jokes, including sexual comments that make others uncomfortable.

The club reserves the right to remove anyone who violates these guidelines.
