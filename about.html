About Us

Lingping Club is a cozy language exchange community based in Bangkok, Chiang Mai, Phuket, and online. We’re a community of curious minds exploring the world through languages, cultures, and shared perspectives.

We are not a language school. Instead, we believe in learning through sharing, meaningful conversations, and real human connections. Our members take turns teaching and learning from one another. Rather than following textbooks, we create a friendly space for cultural exchange and thought-provoking discussions.

Today, our community brings together people from many different language backgrounds — from Thai, English, and Chinese to Japanese, Spanish, Russian, and more. We host a variety of activities such as multilingual discussion groups, book clubs, dinners and cultural exchange events.

We meet in cozy small-group settings and value depth, curiosity, and conversations that go beyond small talk.

Beyond Thailand, Lingping Club also hosts quarterly offline events across several cities in China, including Chengdu, Changsha, Wuhan, Shenzhen, Guangzhou, and more — allowing members to connect across cultures, cities, and perspectives.

At its heart, Lingping Club is a global community built around curiosity, cultural exchange, and lifelong learning.
